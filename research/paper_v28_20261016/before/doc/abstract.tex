Technology-independent logic optimization does not necessarily minimize standard-cell area. We present \textbf{ePMapping}, a post-mapping optimizer under the input delay bound. Joint AO21 chain transformations share factors across roots. Local rewriting combines cell matching, database lookup, Shannon/Davio synthesis and bounded equality saturation. Progressive extraction builds reusable arrival-independent profiles; live pricing accounts for physical phase reuse. Rebuilt-netlist checks enforce area and timing constraints. We evaluate common-input optimization against ABC, recovery after AIG preprocessing, and operator ablations. Across sixteen preprocessed designs, selected outputs change mean area and delay by \OwnArea\% and \OwnDelay\%. Construction reuse reduces repeated work. Deadline selections retain independently verified checkpoints and remain marked incomplete; cold and warm measurements are reported separately.
