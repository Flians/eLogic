\section{Conclusion}
ePMapping combines cut decompositions, arrival-independent profiles, live phase pricing and shared AO21 blocks under a fixed initial-delay bound. The experiments show further mapped-area recovery, but candidate diversity can increase timeouts rather than improve returned results. Exact construction reuse and verified checkpoints reduce repeated work and preserve legal partial progress. Cache capacity trades memory for fewer rebuilds; bounded search still limits candidate coverage on larger designs.
